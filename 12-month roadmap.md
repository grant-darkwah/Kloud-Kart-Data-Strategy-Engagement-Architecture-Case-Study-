Forecasting starts only after two months of matching numbers.|

**Not on this roadmap:** a data lake or lakehouse, live data streams, a cloud warehouse, forecasting, until a Quarter 4 limit is crossed or the numbers have matched for two months.

**If pressure rises** (earlier deadline, faster growth): Quarter 1 moves up and the order stays the same: customer data, then the shop, then the numbers.

**Assumed, to confirm:** the second server is big enough; Nigeria has not yet started collecting customer details (if it has, that legal check moves into Quarter 1); hires start on the dates shown, and if the engineer starts after Mon 1 Feb, later dates slide by the same time.
